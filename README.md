🍲 Taste of Ooru – Kongu Nadu Local Food Marketplace

Taste of Ooru is a local food marketplace startup concept designed to connect customers with nearby restaurants, food shops and home bakers around N.M. Sungam and Pollachi.

Our goal is to promote local businesses and make discovering and ordering delicious local food easier.

🌟 Features

🏪 Discover local restaurants, shops and home bakers.

🍛 Explore meals, biryani, snacks, bakery items and cakes.

🔍 Search and filter shops by food category.

📋 View shop menus and sample prices.

🛒 Add food items to a shopping cart.

📱 Place order requests through WhatsApp.

🎨 Kongu Nadu-inspired design and branding.

📱 Responsive layout for mobile and desktop.

🛠️ Technologies Used

HTML5

CSS3

JavaScript

Google Fonts

Unsplash Images

WhatsApp Click-to-Chat

🚀 How to Run

Download or clone this repository.

Open Taste_of_Ooru.html in a web browser.

Explore shops and food categories.

Add items to your cart.

Enter customer details and open the WhatsApp order links.

💡 Project Vision

To empower small local food businesses, support home entrepreneurs and bring the authentic taste of Kongu Nadu closer to customers.

📍 Target Area

N.M. Sungam and Pollachi, Tamil Nadu, India.

⚠️ Disclaimer

This is a startup presentation prototype. Shop listings, contact details and menu prices are demonstration data and should be verified before real-world use. WhatsApp order links prepare messages for customer review; customers must press Send themselves. This prototype does not provide automatic delivery assignment or payment processing.
