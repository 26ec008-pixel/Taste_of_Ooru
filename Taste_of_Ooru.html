How Taste of Ooru works

🔎

1. Discover

Browse nearby food shops, snacks and home bakers. Check their listed specialities.

🛒

2. Build your order

Add available listed items to your cart, or message a seller to ask for their current menu.

💬

3. Confirm on WhatsApp

Your order details open as a WhatsApp message to each selected shop. The seller confirms delivery and payment with you.

Presentation demo: Menu items and prices shown are sample estimates for the startup presentation, not verified live shop menus. Confirm actual availability and pricing with each seller.
Delivery note: Taste of Ooru connects you directly with sellers. Each shop decides whether it can deliver to your address, the delivery fee, preparation time and payment method. WhatsApp opens a pre-filled message; you must press Send in WhatsApp. It does not send messages automatically.
